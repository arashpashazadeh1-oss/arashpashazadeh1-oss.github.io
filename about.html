Professional Profile

Engineering design + research + computation.

My professional path spans civil and hydraulic design work in consulting and hydropower environments, followed by doctoral research focused on systems-level infrastructure behavior. Across these roles, I have worked with hydraulic structures, pump stations, water and drainage networks, concrete and steel systems, hydropower facilities, hydrologic and hydraulic models, computational methods, and data-driven engineering.

My current research extends this foundation into coastal and floating infrastructure, experimental hydrodynamics, CFD/OpenFOAM modeling, sensor-based motion reconstruction, IMU signal processing, mooring response, and resilient engineering systems. Earlier research emphasized multiple-inflow river routing, nonlinear Muskingum models, artificial neural networks, gene expression programming, statistical analysis, and optimization.
