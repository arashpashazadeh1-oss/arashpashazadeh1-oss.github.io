About

A civil engineer building bridges between engineering practice and academic research.

My academic and professional background combines civil engineering fundamentals with systems-level thinking, allowing me to approach complex infrastructure problems from both design and research perspectives. I work on problems where structures, water, environment, motion, and data interact.

I am particularly interested in coastal and marine infrastructure, hydraulic and floating systems, structural response under environmental loading, sensor-based data analysis, and resilient engineering design.
