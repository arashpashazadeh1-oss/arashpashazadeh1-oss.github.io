Engineering ideas that move from analysis to real infrastructure.

I am Arash Pashazadeh, a Civil Engineer and Ph.D. Researcher in Systems Engineering. My background spans hydraulic and structural design, hydropower, pump stations, water infrastructure, numerical hydrologic and hydraulic modeling, professional programming for civil engineering applications, network design, and web development, along with current research in coastal and floating systems, CFD, sensing, and experimental hydrodynamics.

This website is also designed and developed by me, built from the ground up with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Cloudflare Workers and D1, GitHub Pages, and Telegram-based media integration. Rather than functioning as a static portfolio, it includes a custom content-management panel, live engineering insights, dynamic project/publication/conference records, media galleries, and built-in CV and portfolio generation.
