How translations work

Static pages already have full Persian, Spanish, and German versions. This panel is only for database-driven projects, research, conferences, and publications. Library items remain in their original English content.

Publication titles may remain in their original published language. You can translate the abstract/status only if you prefer.

After saving a translation, the localized public pages automatically request it from the Cloudflare Worker.
